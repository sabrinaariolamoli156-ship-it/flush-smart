ABOUT THE CAMPAIGN

Why Flush Smart?

Toilets are designed to carry human waste and toilet paper through properly designed sanitation systems. However, many people dispose of other materials by flushing them down the toilet.

Items such as wipes, sanitary products, cotton buds, and plastics can contribute to pipe blockages and sanitation problems.

FLUSH SMART! encourages everyone to practice responsible toilet disposal and help protect public health and the environment.

UNDERSTANDING THE ISSUE

What Happens When We Flush Improperly?

Improper disposal can create problems beyond the bathroom.

01. Pipe Blockages

Non-flushable materials can accumulate inside pipes and cause obstructions in drainage systems.

02. Sewer Overflows

Blocked sewer lines can contribute to wastewater backups and overflows.

03. Environmental Pollution

Poor wastewater management can increase the risk of pollution in rivers and other water bodies.

REMEMBER THIS

What Shouldn't Be Flushed?

These common household items belong in a proper waste bin, not in the toilet.

Wet Wipes Sanitary Pads Cotton Buds Diapers Plastic Wrappers Dental Floss Hair Cooking Oil
BE PART OF THE SOLUTION

How Can We Help?

Good sanitation begins with simple, responsible everyday habits.

01. Use the Trash Bin

Dispose of wipes, sanitary products, and other non-flushable materials properly.

02. Educate Others

Share proper sanitation practices with family members, friends, and your community.

03. Keep Toilets Clean

Maintain clean and sanitary toilet facilities to promote a healthier environment.

04. Practice Responsible Disposal

Remember that not everything that fits inside a toilet should be flushed.
