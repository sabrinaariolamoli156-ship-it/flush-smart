01 / OUR ADVOCACY

A SMALL FLUSH.
A BIG RESPONSIBILITY.

FLUSH SMART! is an advocacy campaign that encourages responsible toilet use and proper waste disposal. It reminds everyone that toilets are designed for human waste and toilet paper, not household garbage.

By practicing proper flushing habits, we can help prevent clogged pipes, protect wastewater systems, and maintain a cleaner and healthier environment.

"What goes down the toilet doesn't simply disappear."
💧
02

UNDERSTANDING THE ISSUE

THE PROBLEM BEHIND THE FLUSH

Many people throw different types of garbage into toilets because they believe that flushing makes the waste disappear. However, non-flushable materials can accumulate inside pipes and contribute to blockages in plumbing and wastewater systems.

What seems like a convenient way to dispose of garbage can become a serious problem for sanitation infrastructure and the environment.

03 / THE CONSEQUENCES

WHEN WASTE GOES WRONG.

Improper flushing can affect sanitation systems, public health, and our surroundings.

🚽

Clogged Pipes

Non-flushable materials can block pipes and interrupt the normal flow of wastewater.

💧

Water Pollution

Improper waste disposal can contribute to contamination when waste enters waterways.

⚠️

Health Risks

Exposed wastewater can create unsanitary conditions and increase disease risks.

🔧

Expensive Repairs

Blocked drainage and sewerage systems may require additional cleaning and maintenance.

🏠

Sewage Overflow

Severe blockages can contribute to wastewater overflowing into homes and public areas.

🌍

Environmental Damage

Poor waste management can affect the cleanliness of our surroundings and waterways.

04 / KNOW WHAT BELONGS IN THE BIN

YOUR TOILET IS NOT
A GARBAGE BIN.

These items should never be flushed. Dispose of them in the appropriate waste bins instead.

✕ Wet wipes and baby wipes
✕ Sanitary pads and tampons
✕ Diapers
✕ Plastic wrappers
✕ Cotton buds and cotton balls
✕ Paper towels
✕ Food scraps
✕ Household garbage

05 / TAKE ACTION

BE PART OF THE SOLUTION.

Protecting sanitation systems starts with simple, responsible everyday habits.

01 — Flush Responsibly

Only flush human waste and toilet paper.

02 — Use Proper Waste Bins

Place garbage bins inside or near the bathroom.

03 — Educate Others

Share proper flushing habits with family and friends.

04 — Protect Our Waterways

Practice responsible waste disposal to help prevent pollution.

05 — Report Sanitation Problems

Inform the proper authorities about drainage problems and sewage overflows.

PROTECTING OUR ENVIRONMENT.

These Philippine laws provide frameworks for proper waste management, clean water, and public sanitation.

REPUBLIC ACT NO. 9003

Ecological Solid Waste Management Act of 2000

Promotes proper solid waste management and prohibits certain improper waste disposal practices.
